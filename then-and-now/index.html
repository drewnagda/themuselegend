III Legend × Pharrell, then and now

They had made songs together before. Never a whole album.

05Same writer, 13 years apart

Then

Aim HighJohn Legend · written and produced by Pharrell · 2013

Now

FirefliesJohn Legend feat. Pharrell Williams · Muse, track 14

Before Muse, Pharrell had written and produced one John Legend song, Aim High on Love in the Future. Thirteen years on, Fireflies is credited to Pharrell alone.

“We had done a song for Love in the Future, but we had never really worked this deeply on a project.”

John Legend Mina SayWhat, Sep 28, 2026

Source: Roc Nation · Love in the Future · Apple Music credits

IV What the credits say

The official credits went live with the album on October 23. Across all sixteen tracks they list no samples and no interpolations. The album is produced in full by Pharrell Williams.

The writers are the inner circle: Pharrell, John (as John Stephens), Samm Henshaw, Kawan Prather, Wayne Hector and SWEATA, with Clipse’s Terrence and Gene Thornton on Bodies On The Floor. Fireflies is Pharrell’s alone. Arrangements are credited to Larry Gold, Steven Tirpak, Matt Jones and Terrace Martin, who handled the horns.

So the roots on this page are influences, not samples. Every pairing comes from something John, Pharrell or the label said on the record.
