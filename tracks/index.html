Track by Track

Sixteen songs.
What’s on the record.

All sixteen are out. Under each song: who wrote it, who produced it and who plays on it, read from the official Apple Music credits on release night, plus what John and the label have said about it, in their own words. No sample or interpolation is credited on any track.

Side A
  1. 01

    Muse feat. Pharrell Williams

    The title track opens the album and “gives Muse its spine”: a tumultuous relationship, sketched from the romance of Paris and high art. The name reaches back to the nine Muses of Greek mythology, who governed music, science, literature and the arts.

    “We conjured a character we could write about, freely.”

    John Legend Roc Nation, Oct 7, 2026

    In his own words

    “Oh, it’s an artist and their muse. And it’s partly about their relationship, it’s partly about the artist’s journey.”

    John Legend NPR, Morning Edition, Oct 22, 2026

    “So it ends up feeling like it’s neither one of us specifically.”

    John Legend NPR, Morning Edition, Oct 22, 2026

    “There was no particular, explicit person … We conjured a character we could write about, freely.”

    John Legend Roc Nation, Oct 7, 2026

    “We’ve both been married for over a decade. We both have four kids.”

    John Legend Roc Nation, Oct 7, 2026
    Written by
    Pharrell Williams and John Stephens
    Produced by
    Pharrell Williams
    In the credits
    John on piano; Pharrell on additional vocals; horns arranged by Terrace Martin, who also plays alto sax.

    Listen on Spotify

    Source: Apple Music credits · Roc Nation

  2. 02

    Pray For Ya

    The first taste of Muse happened on a runway: its “rollicking, gospel-tinged groove” soundtracked Louis Vuitton’s Fall-Winter 2026 Men’s show. John was in the room when the orchestra started playing chords he recognized, then the beat dropped. One of the first songs out of the sessions.

    “I hear the orchestra playing some chords, and then I’m like, I recognize those chords.”

    John Legend Elvis Duran and the Morning Show, Oct 9, 2026

    In his own words

    “I hear the beat drop for one of the songs that are on the album … and it was epic.”

    John Legend Elvis Duran and the Morning Show, Oct 2026
    Written by
    Pharrell Williams, John Stephens, Kawan Prather and Samm Henshaw
    Produced by
    Pharrell Williams
    In the credits
    Usher sings background vocals, next to Pharrell and Natalie Imani. Quintin Gulledge on Rhodes and Hammond organ; strings arranged by Larry Gold and Steven Tirpak; horns arranged by Terrace Martin.

    Listen on Spotify

    Source: Apple Music credits · Roc Nation · Elvis Duran and the Morning Show

  4. 04

    Her feat. Pharrell Williams

    Pulls from the doo-wop and gospel John’s father raised him on (his dad sang and played drums; his mother directed the church choir), distilled into a tender groove about falling head over heels.

    “It’s very nostalgic, very romantic. There’s a sweetness to this record. It’s a portrait of infatuation.”

    John Legend Roc Nation, Oct 7, 2026
    Written by
    Pharrell Williams, John Stephens, Kawan Prather and Samm Henshaw
    Produced by
    Pharrell Williams and John Stephens
    In the credits
    John is credited as additional producer here, beside Pharrell. Quintin Gulledge on electric guitar; strings conducted by Larry Gold and Matt Jones.

    Listen on Spotify

    Source: Apple Music credits · Roc Nation

  5. 05

    Daylight

    The song that started it all. Pharrell wrote it with Samm Henshaw and sent it over; John cut it at home in LA around Christmas 2024. Credited writers: Pharrell Williams, Samm Henshaw, Wayne Hector, John Stephens. No sample or interpolation credited.

    Written by
    Pharrell Williams, Samm Henshaw, Wayne Hector and John Stephens
    Produced by
    Pharrell Williams
    In the credits
    The background choir is the room itself: John, Samm Henshaw, Kawan Prather, Terrace Martin, Quintin Gulledge, Jessyca Wilson and Natalie Imani. Samm Henshaw also plays acoustic guitar.

    Listen on Spotify

    Source: Roc Nation · Apple Music credits · Genius

    How Daylight was made

    Before

    A Hook for Clipse

    Pharrell produced every song on Clipse’s comeback album, Let God Sort Em Out. On “The Birds Don’t Sing,” John sang the hook. His voice on that record is what gave Pharrell the idea of building an album around it.

    “I think he loved hearing my voice singing what he had written for that track.”

    John Legend, The Breakfast Club

    Universal Music Canada · The Breakfast Club · Roc Nation

    Late 2024 · Before dawn

    The Call

    A few months later the phone rang in Los Angeles. Pharrell was calling from Paris.

    “It’s like 5 am in LA and I don’t answer.”

    John Legend, Roc Nation album bio

    “I called him after I woke up and he’s like, ‘What are you doing for your next album?’”

    John Legend, Roc Nation album bio

    Roc Nation · NPR

    The invitation

    Come to Paris

    “I’d love for you to spend a few days with me and see what we come up with.”

    Pharrell Williams, as John tells it to NPR

    “He was like, man, we should just do a whole album together.”

    John Legend, The Breakfast Club

    NPR Morning Edition · The Breakfast Club

    Not long after

    The Song Arrives

    Pharrell sent over a song he had written with the singer and songwriter Samm Henshaw. It was called “Daylight.” The finished credits list four writers: Pharrell Williams, Samm Henshaw, Wayne Hector and John himself, credited as John Roger Stephens.

    Roc Nation · Power 98 · Apple Music credits

    Christmas 2024 · Los Angeles

    Cut at Home

    John recorded it at home in Los Angeles, around Christmas. Hearing it, he knew what the album could be.

    “…if this is what the album can sound like, then we are really in for something…”

    John Legend, Roc Nation album bio

    Roc Nation · Power 98

    Then · Paris

    “…getting to the corner office and there’s a piano and a music studio there.”

    John Legend, NPR Morning Edition

    “Sometimes I would do remote sessions with them. I had my studio in L.A. and we would Zoom.”

    John Legend, The Breakfast Club

    NPR Morning Edition · The Breakfast Club · Roc Nation

    September 8, 2026

    First Light

  3. 12

    Bodies On The Floor feat. Clipse

    John wrote the lyric with Samm Henshaw while watching the crime drama MobLand, letting its gang-war language bleed into a toxic, magnetic love story. His own reference point: “Again” and “Another Again” from his second album, Once Again. And Clipse, whose record started all this, return.

    “I wanted to write songs filled with tension and friction.”

    John Legend Roc Nation, Oct 7, 2026

    In his own words

    “Part of me was writing with literal gang wars in mind, a vicious cycle of harm and retaliation.”

    John Legend Roc Nation, Oct 7, 2026

    “…these two lovers hurt each other, betray each other, but there is a heat that brings them back to each other.”

    John Legend Roc Nation, Oct 7, 2026

    “It reminds me of ‘Again’ and ‘Another Again’ from my second album Once Again.”

    John Legend Roc Nation, Oct 7, 2026

    “…which is funny because my actual love life is quite stable and harmonious these days.”

    John Legend Roc Nation, Oct 7, 2026
    Written by
    Pharrell Williams, Terrence Thornton, Gene Thornton, John Stephens and Samm Henshaw
    Produced by
    Pharrell Williams
    In the credits
    Clipse are credited as performers and as writers under their own names, Terrence and Gene Thornton. Terrace Martin plays alto, tenor and baritone sax and arranged the horns.

The players

The same hands run through the whole record. Strings: Larry Gold (conductor and arranger), Matt Jones (conductor and arranger) and Steven Tirpak (arranger). Horns: Terrace Martin (arranger, saxophones), Danny Janklow (saxophones, flute), Brandyn Philips (trumpet, flugelhorn) and Devon Taylor (trombone, tuba, sousaphone). Keys and guitar: Quintin Gulledge. Voices: Jessyca Wilson and Natalie Imani. Pharrell Williams is the producer on every track that carries a producer credit (the three short intros list none, and John is credited as additional producer on Her). No sample or interpolation is credited anywhere on the album.

Credits as listed on Apple Music. Tracklist, titles and sides as printed on johnlegend.com
