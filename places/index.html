I

Springfield

Home, church and the choir loft

011978

Springfield, Ohio

Where it starts

John Roger Stephens is born in Springfield on December 28, 1978. His father works at the International Harvester plant and plays drums; John is at the piano by age four.

Source Wikipedia: John Legend

02Age 7

Springfield, Ohio

The choir loft

His mother sings and directs the church choir, his grandmother plays the organ, and at seven John is singing with the choir himself.

“Our ancestors are our muses. My music has always been me channeling the ancestors.”

John Legend, on the album

Source Wikipedia: John Legend Roc Nation: Muse bio

II

The road east

Penn, the weekend church gig, Lauryn, New York

03Age 16

University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia

Penn at sixteen

Salutatorian at Springfield North, he enrolls at Penn at 16 and becomes president and musical director of the a cappella group the Counterparts.

Source Wikipedia: John Legend

04Late 1990s

Scranton, Pennsylvania

The weekend church gig

To pay his way through school he plays piano at a church in Scranton every weekend. A choir member there knows Lauryn Hill from high school.

Source Billboard Roc Nation: Muse bio

051998

New Jersey

Lauryn Hill

That choir member brings him to Jersey to meet Lauryn Hill. She hires the Penn student to play piano on Everything Is Everything.

Source Billboard Wikipedia: John Legend

06After Penn

New York City

The city

After Penn he works as a management consultant while writing and recording his own music, and sells his first two records at his own shows.

“The hip hop that I used to party to when I was in college and when I first moved to New York.”

John Legend, Elvis Duran Show, Oct 2026

Source Wikipedia: John Legend Elvis Duran Show

072014

Selma, Alabama

Glory

He writes Glory with Common for the film Selma. It wins the Golden Globe and the Oscar, performed on a set of the Edmund Pettus Bridge.

Source Wikipedia: Glory

III

The people in the credits

Where the Muse collaborators come from

081992

Virginia Beach, Virginia

The Neptunes

Pharrell and Chad Hugo meet at a summer band camp and form The Neptunes in 1992. Clipse, the brothers on Bodies On The Floor, come up beside them.

Source Wikipedia: Pharrell Williams Wikipedia: Let God Sort Em Out

09Age 9

Chattanooga, Tennessee

Another choir kid

Usher, also born in 1978, joins his church youth choir in Chattanooga at nine, directed by his mother. On Muse he sings background on Pray For Ya.

Source Wikipedia: Usher Apple Music: Pray For Ya

101991

Atlanta, Georgia

Kawan Prather

Kawan “KP” Prather starts in 1991 as DJ for Parental Advisory, part of the Dungeon Family, then works A&R at LaFace. He was A&R on John’s Evolver in 2008 and co-writes five Muse songs.

Source Wikipedia: Kawan Prather Wikipedia: Evolver Apple Music: Muse credits

112020

Hampton Roads, Virginia

Voices of Fire

Pharrell and his uncle, Bishop Ezekiel Williams, search his home region for gospel voices. The choir they build sings on Fireflies.

Source Wikipedia: Voices of Fire Apple Music: Fireflies

12Daylight

London

Samm Henshaw

Samm Henshaw, raised in South London by Nigerian parents, writes Daylight with Pharrell, the song that started Muse. He has writing credits on six Muse songs.

Source Wikipedia: Samm Henshaw Roc Nation: Muse bio Apple Music: Daylight

13Dec 28, 1978

Los Angeles

Terrace Martin

Terrace Martin, who learned his horn at Santa Monica High and Locke High and arranged the horns on Muse, was born the very same day as John.

Source Wikipedia: Terrace Martin Apple Music: Muse

IV

Making Muse

A 5 AM call, a home studio, a studio in Paris

14Christmas 2024

Los Angeles

The 5 AM call

Pharrell calls from Paris before dawn, LA time: what are you doing for your next album? He sends Daylight, and John cuts it at home around Christmas.

“If this is what the album can sound like, then we are really in for something.”

John Legend, on hearing Daylight

Source Roc Nation: Muse bio The Breakfast Club

152025

Paris

The studio at Louis Vuitton

Muse comes together in Pharrell’s studio inside Louis Vuitton’s Paris headquarters, with a view of the Seine, then over Zoom and more trips.

Source Roc Nation: Muse bio The Breakfast Club

162026

Paris

First taste, on the runway

The world first hears Muse at a fashion show: Pray For Ya soundtracks Louis Vuitton’s Fall Winter 2026 men’s show.

Source Roc Nation: Muse bio
